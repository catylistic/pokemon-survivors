POKéMON SURVIVORS

Pick a region and a partner. Survive 15 minutes, then beat the region's legendary to unlock the next one.

Move: WASD / arrow keys, a controller, hold the mouse, or drag the touch joystick. Attacks fire on their own.
Type matchups matter: super effective hits do 2× (or 4×), resisted hits do ½×. Enemies' attacks follow the chart too.
STAB: moves that share your Pokémon's type do 1.5× damage.
Evolve: you evolve at Lv 12 and Lv 28. Max a move to Lv 6 while holding its type item to unlock its evolved form.
Shiny Pokémon drop Poké Balls. Grab one to catch it as an ally (up to 4).
Pause: Esc, P, the II button or Start. Pick level-up options with 1 / 2 / 3 (or the stick + A). M mutes sound.
